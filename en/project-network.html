P-03Personal project, built during an internship

Three-tier network model

A hierarchical enterprise architecture modelled in Cisco Packet Tracer, inspired by a real site, with redundancy at every level.

  • 2C8200L edge routers, for redundancy
  • 9C9200 access switches, each linked to two distribution switches
  • 4layers: edge, core, distribution, access
01

The context

A hierarchical enterprise architecture modelled in Cisco Packet Tracer, inspired by a real site. Real host names, addressing and access details were replaced with generic values. A personal project, carried out during an internship.

02

The thinking: one layer, one role

LayerEquipmentRole
Edge2 C8200L routersConnect the company to the Internet, so one failure does not cut the site off
CoreC9300X switch stackCarries traffic between floors
DistributionC3850 stackHandles inter-VLAN routing and access rules
Access9 C9200 switchesEach linked to two distribution switches: if one link drops, traffic takes the other
03

The chosen architecture

04

Testing the redundancy

Cut a link between an access switch and the distribution layer: traffic switches to the second link. Cut both and the switch is isolated. That is exactly what dual-homing is designed to prevent.

Simulator · dual-homing
05

What I take from it

No single failure should cut the site off.

Every level is doubled: two routers at the edge, two links for every access switch. Redundancy is decided when the architecture is drawn.
