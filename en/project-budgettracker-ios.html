P-06Personal project

BudgetTracker for iOS

A budgeting app in CFA francs, designed for Mobile Money and written in Swift. I built it on my own, from architecture to security audit, then fixed what the audit found.

  • 48unit tests, all passing

01

The context

A budgeting app in CFA francs, designed for Mobile Money and written in Swift. I built it on my own, from architecture to security audit.

02

The audit and what I did with it

Once the app was written, I audited it against the OWASP Mobile Top 10, the way I would for a client. Four flaws came up. I fixed three and documented the fourth.

OWASP Mobile flawResponseSeverityDecision
M2, insecure storageAES-256-GCM encryption, key in the iOS KeychainHighFixed
M4, insufficient authenticationFace ID or Touch ID required on launchHighFixed
M8, platform tamperingJailbreak detection, blocked if detectedMediumFixed
M9, reverse engineeringResidual risk accepted and documentedLowNot addressed
03

Three barriers on launch

Every time it opens, the app first checks the device isn't compromised, then asks for Face ID or Touch ID, and only reads the data with a key protected by the Keychain.

04

What I take from it

An accepted risk gets documented.

Fixing everything isn't always possible or useful. What matters is that the remaining flaw is known, assessed and written down.
